## Processing

Each key's own sound was taken from the Mechvibes files at commit `326252a13e7bef4f1c35d08ef0189b5af6f8ba02`, by key group
(alpha down 10, backspace down 1, enter down 1, modifier down 4, space down 1, tab down 1 takes; at most 10 for alpha keys and 4 for each other group, spread over
the keyboard; any sound with real
clipping, 3 or more samples in a row at full scale, was left out). A zero-phase 30 Hz
high-pass removed DC offset and rumble. `Tools/py/build_pack.py` then trimmed, faded, resampled
to 48 kHz, normalised per tier, generated the soft, hard and slam tiers from the one recorded
force, and encoded CAF/ALAC.
