## Processing

Each key's own sound was taken from the kbsim files at commit `ba103f3b0afa9dab80447aa2e7e2ed80b6bd80e4`, by key group
(alpha down 5, alpha up 1, backspace down 1, backspace up 1, enter down 1, enter up 1, space down 1, space up 1 takes; at most 10 per group, spread over the keyboard; any sound with real
clipping, 3 or more samples in a row at full scale, was left out). A zero-phase 30 Hz
high-pass removed DC offset and rumble. `Tools/py/build_pack.py` then trimmed, faded, resampled
to 48 kHz, normalised per tier, generated the soft, hard and slam tiers from the one recorded
force, and encoded CAF/ALAC.
